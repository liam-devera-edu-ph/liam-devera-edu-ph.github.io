REFLECTION


Looking back at all our activities this term, I know how much my communication skills have improved both in schoolwork and in real-life situations. Working on the Panaderia Delight project taught me that turning ideas into practical marketing strategies is important for a business to grow. Outside of group tasks, handling email inquiries with support services showed me that it is important to be direct and complete with my details to avoid unnecessary delays. On a personal experience, recording my video presentations and writing my application materials helped me spot areas where I could speak more naturally and present my experiences better.
Balancing my life as a student-athlete with these business tasks wasn't always easy, but I learned discipline and time management. Analyzing brand cases like SK-II also helped me understand how continuous innovation and focusing on customer value drive long-term business success. Reflecting on these tasks made me pay attention to how I deliver information, whether through written reports, visual design, or face to face interactions. I also learned that receiving feedback is a important part of growing and making my future outputs even better. Every assignment challenged me to adapt my communication style depending on who I was talking to. These activities made me more confident, organized, and aware of how I interact with others in school and real-life.
